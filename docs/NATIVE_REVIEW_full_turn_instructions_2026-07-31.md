# Native-review target list — under-specified full-turn role-play instructions (2026-07-31)

fa-en / prs-en dialogue_response & speak_response exercises whose ONLY accepted answer is a
multi-turn block (>=2 sentences) but whose L1 instruction does NOT tell the learner to produce
the whole turn (nl-en's sibling instruction does). A learner giving a partial answer is graded
wrong. FIX: rewrite the fa/prs instruction to mirror nl-en's 'produce the whole turn: X, Y, Z'.
Confirmed example: en.a2.unit25.lesson04.exercise05.reply_as_daniel.
